# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

VibeDraw is for AI workshop attendees who are learning how language, image-generation, and vision models can cooperate in an agentic loop. They need to see the intermediate reasoning artifacts clearly while a slow, paid workflow is running.

## Product Purpose

The app turns an abstract concept into a sequence of progressively refined images. Success means a participant can follow every prompt, image, critique, and stop condition and understand why the next iteration changed.

## Positioning

VibeDraw makes iterative multimodal improvement visible instead of hiding it behind a one-click generation result.

## Brand Personality

Focused, luminous, and instructive. The interface should feel like a creative light table used during a live technical demonstration.

## Anti-references

Do not present the workflow as a generic chatbot conversation. Avoid message bubbles, assistant avatars, and conversational filler that obscure the ordered model loop.

## Design Principles

- Keep the evolving image visually dominant.
- Expose every meaningful input and evaluation without exposing credentials.
- Show progress incrementally; never make participants wait behind an unexplained loading state.
- Preserve honest stop reasons and the best result when scores regress.
- Use color to distinguish stages, always reinforced by explicit labels.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide solid desktop usability: readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, standard controls, and status labels that do not rely on color alone. A specialized high-accessibility mode is outside the demo scope.
